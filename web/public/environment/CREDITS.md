# Balcony scene — asset credits

## Balcony scene

The balcony room (`写实花园室内.blend`) was provided by the project owner. Its texture names identify paid libraries:
CGAxis PBR, Quixel Megascans, Evermotion Archmodels 101, plus stock textures (see
[balcony assessment](../../../docs/design/balcony-assessment.md)). Everything in `balcony-toon/` is rendered or
exported from that scene, except `book.glb`, a book modelled for this project, and `nap.json`, `nap-walk.json` and
`nap-back.json`, her nap's pose and walks, animated for this project (all MIT). The project owner confirmed the licence for web use on 2026-11-09, and on 2026-11-10 chose
to share the renders in this repository: the `.webp` layers, `scene.json` and `book.glb` are included, the
renders © the project owner; included so this project runs; you may use them to run and develop this project, not reuse them elsewhere or redistribute them separately; not covered by the MIT licence. The scene's chair and plant meshes
(`chair-visible.glb`, `plants.glb`) stay local and git-ignored; the page rebuilds stand-ins for them
(`/standin/CREDITS.md`).
